## Chibi party (2026-11-09)

The owner's kids found the 28 px adult heroes hard to connect with, so the party was redrawn as cute chibi animals and
approved on 2026-11-09. The adult `unit_*` rows above are superseded: their raw images were removed (they are in git
history) and the tiles and the rock prop keep using their rows. Each sprite's full build chain is in its
`tools/art/spec/sprites.json` entry; the lion, red panda and bunny are converted from these images, while the snow
leopard, lamb and golem are drawn in code (`tools/art/draw_chibi.py`) from them, because pale and glowing designs turn
to speckle when converted. Earlier rounds that were not kept are not listed here.
