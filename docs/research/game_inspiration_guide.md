## 🔍 Deep-Dive Study Breakdown & Links

### 1. ⚡ "Juice It or Lose It" (Martin Jonasson & Petri Purho)
* **Link**: 🎬 [Juice It Or Lose It - GDC Talk](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Fy0aQCDmgnxg)
* **Why Watch**: The foundational talk on how screen shake, hit-stop, particle bursts, pitch shifts, and squash-and-stretch transform a simple prototype into a vibrant game.

### 2. 🩸 Dead Cells: What the F*n!? (Motion Twin)
* **Link**: 🎬 [Dead Cells: What the F*n!? - GDC Talk](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vVj_L04K9h0)
* **Link**: 📄 [Anatomy of Game Juice Article](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/the-anatomy-of-game-juice)
* **Why Watch/Read**: Explains how Motion Twin used "coyote time", input queuing, 3D-to-2D sprite sheet rendering, and 0.03s hit-pause frames to make melee attacks feel intensely satisfying.

### 3. 🏛️ Hades: Developing Hell (Supergiant Games & Noclip)
* **Link**: 🎬 [Hades: Developing Hell - Noclip Full Documentary Series](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PL-THgg8QnvU4JEVov1tMlFThNYS92F8uC)
* **Why Watch**: Follows Supergiant Games over 2 years showing how they designed the 4-slot ability system, weapon aspect promotions, and readable boss telegraphs.

### 4. 🗡️ Hyper Light Drifter: Applying 3D Design to 2D (Heart Machine)
* **Link**: 🎬 [3D Level Design Skills in 2D World of Hyper Light Drifter](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9_L5X0U8NqA)
* **Why Watch**: Explains how Heart Machine created high-contrast pixel art silhouettes, fluid 60fps dash animations, and evocative environmental color palettes.

### 5. 💀 Death's Door: Developer Deep-Dive (Acid Nerve)
* **Link**: 🎬 [Unity Creator Spotlight: Death's Door by Acid Nerve](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Nn7Z8uX_14k)
* **Why Watch**: Shows how Acid Nerve designed clean, readable boss arenas, 0.8s telegraph windups, and snappy sword slashes.

### 6. 🌐 CrossCode: 7 Years of Action RPG Development (Radical Fish Games)
* **Link**: 📄 [Radical Fish Games Devblog](https://www.cross-code.com/en/blog)
* **Why Read**: Demonstrates how on-the-fly elemental swapping (Heat, Cold, Shock, Wave) works for both combat combos and overworld puzzle dungeon gating.
